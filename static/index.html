SIIR explorer demo company

how to use

The explorer runs entirely in your browser: it fetches one GraphQL snapshot from the public Acki Nacki mirror node, decodes the contract's persistent state on the fly (no server, no tvm-cli), and reconstructs every getter.

Paste a dapp_id::account_id address in the bar (factory, marketplace, or company — the demo above) or open a company through the factory directory.
